Delete your DynamiteArena account

DynamiteArena by Main Mode Studio (Ömer Faruk İncibaş) · Last updated: 28 October 2026

DynamiteArena creates an anonymous player account the first time you play online. It has no password and no email address: your phone proves it owns the account with a key stored only on that phone. You can delete the account and the data tied to it at any time, in the game or by email. There is no waiting period and no deactivation step: the account is deleted.

1. Delete it in the game

  1. Open DynamiteArena and tap Settings (the gear).
  2. Open the About tab, then Player account → Open.
  3. Tap Delete, read what will be deleted, then hold the red button until it fills.

The phone needs an internet connection. When our server confirms the deletion, the game also removes the account key, the account record and its saved copies from your phone and starts again from the first screen.

2. Ask us by email

If you can't open the game (for example you uninstalled it), email us. You don't need to reinstall anything.

To: farukincibas@gmail.com
Subject: DynamiteArena account deletion
Include: your Player ID (such as #1234) and your support code. Both are shown in Settings → About → Player account.

The Player ID alone is not enough, because anyone can see it; the support code shows the account is yours. If you no longer have the support code, tell us everything you remember (Player ID, roughly when you played, your player name) and we will check the request by hand. We may ask a follow-up question before deleting. We answer within 30 days, usually much sooner.

3. What is deleted

  • The player account: its ID, the public part of the phone's key, the hashed support code, age group, terms acceptance and player name.
  • Everything the account holds on our server: gold, diamonds, owned characters and items, Campaign rewards, daily gift progress and its operation history.

4. What is kept, and for how long

  • A deletion record: a one-way hash of the deleted account's ID and the date. It holds nothing that identifies you; we keep it so that restoring a backup can never bring a deleted account back.
  • Backups: server backups are kept for at most 30 days. If a backup is restored, every deletion in the deletion record is applied again before the game goes back online.
  • Purchase records: if in-app purchases are offered and you made one, the store's purchase record is kept without the link to your account, where tax, refund or chargeback rules require it.
  • On your phone: Campaign levels you have cleared and your settings stay on the phone until you uninstall the game. They were never sent to our server.

5. Accounts we delete on our own

Accounts that were created but never used again are deleted after 30 days. Accounts that have not connected for 24 months are deleted.
