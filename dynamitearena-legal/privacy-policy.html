Privacy Policy

DynamiteArena · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game DynamiteArena ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it. DynamiteArena has a single-player Campaign and an optional online Multiplayer mode. The Campaign works without an account and without sending your game data to us. We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number.

1. Information We Collect

Stored only on your device

Your progress, stars, lives, gold and diamonds, owned characters, settings and player name are saved on your phone. On first launch the game asks for your birth month and year; it keeps only your age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over) and never stores your birth date. Your answers to the consent and Terms screens are stored on the device too.

When you play online (Multiplayer)

Multiplayer runs on servers we operate. Before your first online match the game asks you to accept the Terms of Service. When you play online we process:

DataWhy
A random player account: an account ID, the public part of a key created on your phone, and a Player ID such as #1234 To recognise your phone when it connects again, without a password or email. The private key never leaves your phone.
A support code, stored on our side only in a hashed form So you can prove the account is yours when you contact us (for example to delete it).
Your player name, the character you choose, and your moves during a match To run the match. Other players in the same match see your name, tag and character.
Your age group and the Terms version you accepted To apply age-appropriate rules and to know which Terms you agreed to.
Account state (gold, diamonds, owned characters, Campaign stars) when it is kept on our servers To keep purchases and rewards safe from loss and cheating.
Your IP address and connection timing Needed to connect at all, and to limit abuse (for example too many new accounts from one network). We do not use it to locate you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show a rewarded advertisement that you choose to watch for an extra life. Google AdMob processes your advertising ID, device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with the ad. The ad system starts only after the age screen has been answered. For players under 13, ad requests are marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, so no personalised advertising is shown; for players aged 13–17 they are marked as under the age of consent.

Your privacy choices (Google User Messaging Platform)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent tool asks for your choice before advertising data is used. You can change it later in Settings → Privacy choices.

Google Play

The game may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating window. Ratings and reviews you write are handled by Google; we do not receive them through the game.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the Settings screen, your email app opens. We then receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use them only to answer you.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting service, and it currently has no real-money purchases.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the game and its online matches, and to keep your account and items.
  • To protect players and the game from cheating, abuse and attacks.
  • To show the optional rewarded ad you choose to watch.
  • To answer your messages and requests.

Legal bases (where the GDPR applies): performing our agreement with you (online play and your account), our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure, and your consent for advertising where consent is required.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Other players see your player name, tag and character in matches. Our servers are hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. Google processes the advertising and consent data described above under its own policies: AdMob & privacy, Google Privacy Policy.

4. Retention

  • Data on your device stays until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
  • An online account is kept until you ask us to delete it. Accounts that were created but never used again are deleted after 30 days; accounts that have not connected for 24 months are deleted.
  • Server backups are kept for at most 30 days. When we restore a backup, deletions made since that backup are applied again.
  • If real-money purchases are added in the future, purchase records may be kept after an account is deleted, without the account link, where tax or refund rules require it.

5. Children

DynamiteArena can be played by all ages. The age screen lets us treat younger players differently: players under 13 get child-directed advertising and are not invited to send feedback by email. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's online account deleted, contact us (see section 6 for what to include).

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Because accounts are anonymous, we identify an account by its Player ID (such as #1234) and its support code, both shown in Settings → About → Player account; please include both if you can. If you no longer have them, tell us what you remember and we will check your request by hand. You can also:

  • delete your player account in the game (Settings → About → Player account → Delete) or by email; see Delete your account;
  • change your advertising choices in Settings → Privacy choices (where shown);
  • reset your advertising ID in your device settings;
  • delete everything stored on your phone by uninstalling the game.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Online traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted (DTLS). Your phone proves it owns its account with a private key that never leaves the device. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited data involved.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change matters for online play, the game asks you to accept the updated terms again.
