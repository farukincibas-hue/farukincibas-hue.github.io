Privacy Policy

DynamiteArena · Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game DynamiteArena ("the game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it. DynamiteArena has a single-player Campaign and an optional online Multiplayer mode. You never sign in: the game creates an anonymous player account for you the first time it uses our servers (see below). We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number. If you live in Türkiye, our Turkish privacy notice (Aydınlatma Metni) explains the same processing under the KVKK.

1. Information We Collect

Stored on your device

Your progress, stars, lives, settings and player name are saved on your phone. On first launch the game asks for your birth month and year, never the day. It keeps your age group (under 13, 13–17, or 18 and over) and, for players under 18, the month in which that group next changes (the month after your 13th or 18th birthday month), so it can move you up a group without asking again. Your birth month and year can be worked out from that month, so we use it for nothing else. For players aged 18 and over only the age group is kept. Tapping Continue on that first screen also accepts the Terms of Service; the version you accepted and when you accepted it are stored on your phone. Your choice in Google's consent tool (only in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), your Usage statistics setting and the list of players you block are stored on the device too.

Your player account (our servers)

Some features run on servers we operate: Campaign rewards, the daily gift, the Shop, quests, the inbox, the Rankings and online Multiplayer. The first time the game needs one of them (for example to save the gold and stars you win in the Campaign, to claim the daily gift, to buy in the Shop or to play online), it creates an anonymous player account. Without an internet connection the Campaign still works: what you win waits on your phone until the game can connect. If you have not accepted the current Terms of Service (for example because you answered the first screen in an older version of the game, or the Terms have changed since), the game asks you before anything is sent to our servers. Once the account exists we process:

DataWhy
A random player account: an account ID, the public part of a key created on your phone, and a Player ID such as #1234 To recognise your phone when it connects again, without a password or email. The private key never leaves your phone.
A support code, stored on our side only in a hashed form So you can prove the account is yours when you contact us (for example to delete it).
Your player name, the character, launcher and dynamite look you choose, and your moves during a match To run the match. Other players in the same match see your name, tag, character, launcher, dynamite look and mastery badge. The Rankings show your name, tag and league points to other players.
Your age group (and, if you are under 18, the month in which it next changes) and the Terms version you accepted To apply age-appropriate rules, to move you up a group at the right time and to know which Terms you agreed to.
Account state: gold, diamonds, owned characters and items, Campaign stars and rewards, daily gift, quests, mastery, league, inbox messages and online match results To keep purchases and rewards safe from loss and cheating, and to give quest, mastery and league rewards.
Level results: for Campaign levels and offline Practice matches, the level, whether you won, stars, crates broken, power-ups picked up, time, the character you played and your place To give Campaign rewards and quest, mastery and league progress. Deleted after 21 days.
Level progress events: for every Campaign level you play, when you start it, whether you win or lose and how long it took, when a tutorial is finished, and how far into the level you broke the first crate. Each event carries only its name, the level number, your phone's clock time and that one number To see where players get stuck, above all whether new players get through the tutorial and the first level. They wait on your phone until it can connect, are kept with your account (at most 2,000 events) and are deleted with it.
Player reports: when you report another player's name, the reported name, the match, the seat and that the report came from your account To review offensive names. If we act on a report, we may change that player's name. Your reports are deleted with your account.
Error reports: when something goes wrong in the game (for example a connection that fails or a file that does not load), the game version, the Android version, the phone model, the error text and how many times it happened To find and fix problems. They contain no name, no text you type and no location, and they are sent with your online account so we can count how many players are affected.
Your IP address Needed to connect at all, and to limit abuse (for example too many new accounts from one network). Our lobby server holds it in memory while you are connected. For the new-account limit, the account service receives only a one-way code made from it with a key that changes every day and exists only in the lobby server's memory; the service keeps that code in memory until a day has passed since the last new account from that network. Your IP address is never written to your account, and we do not use it to locate you.
Connection times: when your account (and each phone key) last connected, the days (in UTC) on which your account was created or connected, and your phone's time-zone offset (saved when you claim the daily gift) The last connection recognises your phone and lets us delete unused accounts (after 30 days) and inactive ones (after 24 months). The connection days let us count how many players come back. The time-zone offset runs the daily gift, quests and other daily limits on your local day. All of these are deleted with the account.

Buying diamonds (Google Play)

Diamonds are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment. We never receive your name, email address, card or other payment details. When you buy, the game gives Google Play a random code for your player account (not your Player ID), and our server then asks Google Play about the purchase. We process:

DataWhy
The Google Play order number, the pack bought, when it was bought, its state (completed, waiting, refunded or cancelled) and whether it was a test purchase To add the diamonds you paid for, to handle refunds and chargebacks, and to keep the records that tax and accounting rules require.
The purchase token Google Play creates for the purchase, and the random account code To confirm the purchase with Google Play, to make sure it belongs to your account, and to stop the same purchase from being used twice. The token itself is deleted once the purchase is confirmed; we keep only a one-way fingerprint of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show a rewarded advertisement that you choose to watch for an extra life. The ad system starts only after the first screen has been answered. To show the ad, limit how often it is shown, count it and prevent fraud, Google AdMob processes your advertising ID, device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and your interactions with the ad, and it may store and read information on your phone. It does this for non-personalised ads too.

  • Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (for example in Türkiye), the game asks Google only for non-personalised ads: ads are not chosen from a profile of your interests or of what you do in other apps. No consent window is shown.
  • In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent tool asks for your choice (see Your privacy choices below) and AdMob follows it. Until you answer, only non-personalised ads are requested.
  • For players under 13, ad requests are also marked as child-directed and under the age of consent; for players aged 13–17, as under the age of consent. Players under 18 are never shown personalised ads.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) — players aged 18 and over only

For players aged 18 and over, the game sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: which levels are started, won, lost or left and after how long, which character is used, Arena match results, gold and diamonds earned and spent, whether a rewarded ad finished, visits to the diamond shop and how purchase attempts ended (the pack and the result, such as completed or cancelled). For purchases made through Google Play, Google Analytics also records the pack, its price and currency by itself. Google Analytics also records a random ID for this installation (app-instance ID), the device model, operating system and app version, an approximate location (country or city) derived from your masked IP address, and when and how long the game is open. We use these numbers only to see which levels are too hard or too easy, whether rewards, prices and the Shop are balanced, and whether players come back.

We do not send your player name, Player ID or account ID, and advertising ID collection is turned off for these statistics; they are not used for advertising. For players under 18 no usage statistics are sent.

  • Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (for example in Türkiye), usage statistics are on by default for players aged 18 and over, based on our legitimate interest in improving the game. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → About → Usage statistics; from then on the game sends none.
  • In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, they follow the choice you make in Google's consent tool (below).

Your privacy choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before advertising data and usage statistics are used. You can change it later in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere else, the game shows no consent window: ads are always non-personalised, and players aged 18 and over can turn usage statistics off in Settings → About → Usage statistics.

Google Play

The game may ask Google Play to show its in-app rating window. Ratings and reviews you write are handled by Google; we do not receive them through the game.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the Settings screen, your email app opens. We then receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use them only to answer you.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details: payments are handled by Google Play. The game uses no third-party crash-reporting service: its own error reports (see above) go only to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the game and its online matches, and to keep your account and items.
  • To deliver diamonds you buy, handle refunds, and keep purchase records the law requires.
  • To protect players and the game from cheating, abuse and attacks.
  • To find and fix errors in the game (error reports).
  • To show the optional rewarded ad you choose to watch.
  • To improve the game: to see where players get stuck (level progress events) and how many come back (connection days) and, for players aged 18 and over, which levels are too hard or too easy, whether rewards, prices and the Shop are balanced, and whether players come back (usage statistics).
  • To answer your messages and requests.

Legal bases: performing our agreement with you, the Terms of Service (your account, Campaign rewards, online play and your purchases); our legal obligations (tax and accounting records of purchases, and answering your privacy requests); and our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and working, in improving it and in funding a free game with an optional rewarded ad (abuse limits, player reports, error reports, level progress events, connection days, non-personalised ads and, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, usage statistics). Outside those regions we do not base any processing on consent, and the game shows no consent window. In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, personalised advertising and usage statistics rely on the consent you give in Google's consent tool. If you live in Türkiye, our Turkish privacy notice (Aydınlatma Metni) sets out the same processing and its legal grounds under the KVKK.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Other players see what is listed above (your player name, tag, character and the Rankings). Our servers are hosted by OVH SAS in France. Encrypted backups of the server database are stored with Backblaze, Inc. (B2 Cloud Storage); they are encrypted on our server before they leave it, and Backblaze cannot read them. Google processes purchase payments and the advertising, consent and usage statistics data described above under its own policies, in data centres that may be outside your country, including in the United States: AdMob & privacy, Google Analytics for Firebase data collection, Google Privacy Policy.

4. Retention

  • Data on your device stays until you clear the game's data or uninstall it.
  • An online account is kept until you ask us to delete it. Accounts that were created but never used again are deleted after 30 days; accounts that have not connected for 24 months are deleted.
  • Level results are deleted after 21 days. Level progress events, connection days, the last connection time and the time-zone offset are deleted with the account.
  • Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for at most 14 months.
  • Error reports are deleted 30 days after the error was last seen, and with the account.
  • Server backups are kept for at most 30 days. When we restore a backup, deletions made since that backup are applied again.
  • Purchase records are kept for as long as tax, accounting and refund rules require, also after the account is deleted; deleting the account removes the link between the records and the account.

5. Children

DynamiteArena can be played by all ages. The age screen lets us treat younger players differently: players under 13 get child-directed advertising and are not invited to send feedback by email, and players under 18 are never shown personalised ads and no usage statistics are sent for them. Parents can make every purchase need their approval with Google Play's purchase approval settings (for example Google Family Link). If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's online account deleted, contact us (see section 6 for what to include).

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. Because accounts are anonymous, we identify an account by its Player ID (such as #1234) and its support code, both shown in Settings → About → Player account; please include both if you can. If you no longer have them, tell us what you remember and we will check your request by hand. If you live in Türkiye, the Turkish privacy notice lists your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK. You can also:

  • delete your player account in the game (Settings → About → Player account → Delete) or by email; see Delete your account;
  • turn usage statistics off in Settings → About → Usage statistics (players aged 18 and over, outside the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland);
  • change your advertising and usage-statistics choices in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices (in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland);
  • reset your advertising ID in your device settings;
  • delete everything stored on your phone by uninstalling the game.

Usage statistics are not linked to your Player ID or account, so we cannot look them up for a particular player; uninstalling the game starts a new app-instance ID.

We answer requests within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Türkiye you can apply to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK) after you have written to us.

7. Security

Online traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted (DTLS). Your phone proves it owns its account with a private key that never leaves the device. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited data involved.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. This policy informs you; you do not accept it. When the Terms of Service change, the game asks you to accept the new version before it next uses our servers.
