DynamiteArena

3D dynamite battler · Free · 10 languages

Throw. Bounce. Chain.

A fast, colorful arena battler. Clear the crates, grab power-ups, corner your rivals and be the last one standing.

  • Online four-player arena

    Quick Play drops you into a free-for-all in seconds, or share a room code with friends.

  • A 40-level campaign

    Four chapters of robots, traps and boss fights. Plays offline, anywhere.

  • Fair from the first match

    Everyone starts with one dynamite. Power-ups are picked up in the arena.

  • Friendly by design

    There is no chat at all, so online play stays friendly.

Principles

How the studio works

  1. 01

    Systems before screens

    Rules and economy are written as pure logic, away from the interface. If the model is not interesting on paper, no amount of polish rescues it.

  2. 02

    Free without the fine print

    No paywalled progress, no timers you have to pay to skip. Everything we ship can be finished without opening your wallet.

  3. 03

    Ship small, then sharpen

    One release at a time, sharpened with the people actually playing it. New updates and languages beat a longer backlog.

One person.
Every system.

Main Mode Studio is one person who designs the systems, writes the code, balances the numbers and ships the builds.

That keeps the catalog small on purpose: a release goes out when it still holds up on the tenth run, not the first.

Questions

Good to know

Are the games free?

Yes. Both games are free on Google Play, and everything in them can be finished without paying.

Where can I play?

On Android, through Google Play. Both Wealthbound and DynamiteArena are live now.

Can I play DynamiteArena offline?

Yes. The 40-level campaign plays offline, anywhere. Online four-player battles need a connection.

Is online play friendly for younger players?

DynamiteArena has no chat at all, so online matches are about dynamite, not words.

Who makes these games?

One person. Main Mode Studio designs, codes, balances and ships every game from Türkiye.

I am writing about the games. Where do I start?

The press kit has logos, screenshots and fact sheets. For anything else, email the studio.
