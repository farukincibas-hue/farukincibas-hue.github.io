DynamiteArena

3D dynamite battler

Throw. Bounce. Chain.

A fast, colorful arena battler. Drop dynamite, bounce it off walls and start chain blasts. Clear the crates, grab power-ups, corner your rivals and be the last one standing.

  • Free to play
  • Online four-player
  • 40-level campaign
  • 10 languages
Play on Google Play ↗ Follow on YouTube ↗

Every blast changes the arena.

Crates fall, power-ups drop and the walls close in, so no two matches play out the same way.

How a match works

Throw, bounce, chain.

  1. 01 / THROW

    Pick your launcher

    A high toss by hand, a fast slingshot, a hockey stick that slides it along the floor, or a balloon that floats over walls.

  2. 02 / BOUNCE

    Walls are part of the plan

    Bank your dynamite off a wall or curve it around a corner to reach a rival hiding behind cover.

  3. 03 / CHAIN

    One blast sets off the next

    Line up your dynamite and a single spark tears across the arena. The last one standing wins.

Pick your hero

Every DynamiteArena hero brings a super of their own.

  • Blaster

    Giant dynamite that blasts straight through crates.

  • Magnet

    Dynamite that chases down an enemy.

  • Trapper

    Plants a mine that waits for someone to come close.

  • Guard

    Raises a shield that blocks a hit.

  • Hopper

    Springs out of danger.

  • Sticky

    Gum that slows enemies down.
