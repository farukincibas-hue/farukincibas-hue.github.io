Wealthbound

Money board game

Outrun your expenses. Build your freedom.

Draw a career, move through an unpredictable board and build income-producing assets. Reach financial freedom before your rival, or before life sends your plan off course.

  • Solo or invite a rival

Every choice changes the run.

Careers, opportunities and setbacks turn each board into a new route toward financial freedom.

How a run works

Race, build, adapt.

  1. 01 / RACE

    Freedom has a finish line

    Cover monthly expenses with passive income before your rival gets there.

  2. 02 / BUILD

    Make money work for you

    Balance cash, debt and assets while every opportunity asks for a trade-off.

  3. 03 / ADAPT

    No two runs are the same

    New careers, a different mix of businesses and life events you cannot plan for.
